Referral Commission Calculator

Tiered referral partner commissions. Each sale has a price set by the partner, a fixed HQ software cut comes off the top, and the partner earns a tier-based share of what remains — graduating 40% → 60% → 80% by number of paid referrals. Compares a retroactive bump (top-tier rate applied to every past referral) against a graduated payout.
